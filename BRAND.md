# Ivy Research brand guide

## Logo System

Use the horizontal logo as the default brand signature. In that lockup, the connected-dot mark functions as the `I`, so the text starts with `vy Research`. The top and bottom nodes align vertically to keep the mark legible as an `I` while the middle node preserves the network/research character. Use the mark alone for favicons, app icons, avatars, and compact spaces where the full wordmark would be illegible.

## Variants

- Black: primary use on white or light backgrounds.
- White: inverse use on black or dark backgrounds.
- Stacked: secondary layout for square-ish placements.
- Wordmark-only: spells out `Ivy Research`; use when the mark does not directly precede the text.

## Clear Space

Keep clear space around the horizontal logo at least equal to the diameter of one mark node. Do not place text, borders, or other marks inside that area.

## Minimum Sizes

- Horizontal logo: 160 px wide for screen use.
- Mark: 24 px for favicons and UI; prefer 32 px or larger where possible.
- Print: use SVG whenever possible.

## Color

- Primary black: `#000000`
- Inverse white: `#ffffff`

Do not recolor the mark or wordmark independently. Keep the full logo one color.

## Do Not

- Stretch, skew, outline, or add effects to the logo.
- Recreate the wordmark with live text in production assets.
- Place the black logo on dark backgrounds or the white logo on light backgrounds.
- Change the spacing between the mark and wordmark.
